Scenario: User has all 4 parts of Acceltra Prime in inventory but only wants to keep 1 part for themselves.

🔵 Acceltra Prime Barrel (In Inventory - can sell)
🔵 Acceltra Prime Blueprint (In Inventory - can sell)

Completion Status: 0% (0/4 parts truly owned)

Instructions:
